How the Country Is Organized

Provinces hold cities, and cities hold districts and counties, which is why a Chinese city can include farms and hills.

In this unit you will learn
  • how China is divided into provinces
  • why a Chinese "city" can include farms and mountains
  • what a five-year plan is
  • how big China's high-speed rail network has become
Words to know
Province
A large division of the country, like a US state.
Municipality
Here, a city run like a province, reporting straight to the national government.
Prefecture-level city
A city that also governs the countryside around it.
Five-year plan
The national blueprint of goals for the economy for the next five years.

3.1 Provinces

Mainland China has 31 provincial-level divisions. Most are provinces. Four are big cities that run like provinces, called municipalities: Beijing, Shanghai, Tianjin and Chongqing.

The provinces with the most people are Guangdong (about 128 million), Shandong (about 101 million) and Henan (about 98 million), all at the end of 2024. Each has more people than Germany.

Hangzhou's province, Zhejiang, ranks 7th by population, with 66.7 million at the end of 2024. By the size of its economy it ranks 4th: 9.45 trillion yuan in 2025, behind only Guangdong, Jiangsu and Shandong. So Zhejiang has fewer people than several provinces but more money per person.

Did you know?

Chongqing, one of the four municipalities, is about the size of Austria and is mostly mountains and countryside.

3.2 Boxes inside boxes

Think of China's local government as boxes inside boxes. A province holds cities. Each city holds districts and counties. Each of those holds townships and neighborhoods.

The middle box is the surprise for Americans. A prefecture-level city like Hangzhou governs a large area of countryside as well as the city itself. Hangzhou has 13 parts: 10 urban districts, 1 small city called Jiande, and 2 rural counties, Tonglu and Chun'an.

Worked example

How can Hangzhou have 12.7 million people?

  1. "Hangzhou" covers 16,596 square km. Connecticut covers about 14,357 square km.
  2. So the area called Hangzhou is a bit bigger than the whole state of Connecticut.
  3. Its 12.7 million people include everyone living in those rural counties and hill towns.

The packed city you are walking around is only part of "Hangzhou."

Look for it in Hangzhou

Addresses name the district, such as Xihu District (the West Lake area) or Binjiang District (south of the river).

3.3 Five-year plans

A five-year plan sets targets and priorities for the economy for the next five years. China has issued them since 1953. The current plan, the 15th, covers 2026 to 2030. Provinces and cities write their own five-year plans under it.

Did you know?

Hangzhou's own plan for 2025 listed getting Tsientang University ready as one of the city's projects for the year. Unit 8.7 tells that story.

3.4 High-speed rail

By the end of 2025 China had about 50,400 km of high-speed rail, up from 37,900 km at the end of 2020. That is longer than the rest of the world's high-speed rail combined, and longer than the distance around the Earth. The fastest trains in regular service run at 350 km/h (217 mph).

Worked example

How fast is Shanghai to Hangzhou?

  1. Shanghai Hongqiao station to Hangzhou East is roughly 160 to 175 km, about 100 miles.
  2. The fastest trains take 45 minutes.
  3. New York to Philadelphia is about the same distance.

Imagine making that trip in the time it takes to watch one TV episode.

Did you know?

In 2025, Chinese railways counted 4.26 billion passenger trips, about three for every person in the country.

Check your understanding

  1. Name the four cities that run like provinces.
    Show answer

    Beijing, Shanghai, Tianjin and Chongqing.

  2. Why does Hangzhou's population include farmers in the hills?
    Show answer

    Hangzhou is a prefecture-level city. It governs rural counties and towns around the city, so their people are counted too.

  3. How does Zhejiang rank among provinces by people and by economy?
    Show answer

    7th by people, 4th by economy.

  4. About how long does the fastest train take from Shanghai to Hangzhou?
    Show answer

    About 45 minutes.
