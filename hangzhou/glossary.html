Glossary

All 33 "words to know" from the eight units, in alphabetical order, each with the unit that introduced it.

A C D F G I L M O P R S T W Y

A

ASEAN
A group of Southeast Asian countries that trade together as a bloc. It had 10 members until Timor-Leste joined in October 2025. Unit 1: Asia and Where China Sits
Atrium
A tall open space in the middle of a building, usually lit from above. Unit 8: The City of Hangzhou

C

Capital
The city where the country's rulers and government are based. Unit 4: History in Four Turning Points
Causeway
A raised path or road across water. Unit 8: The City of Hangzhou

D

Delta
Flat land at a river's mouth, built from mud the river drops. Unit 5: The City Map of China
District
One of the parts a Chinese city is divided into for local government. Unit 8: The City of Hangzhou
Dynasty
A family that rules a country for generations, until another family takes over. Unit 4: History in Four Turning Points

F

Five-year plan
The national blueprint of goals for the economy for the next five years. Unit 3: How the Country Is Organized

G

GDP
Gross domestic product: the total value of everything a place produces in a year. Unit 5: The City Map of China

I

Industrial park
A planned area built for factories and companies. Unit 7: Hangzhou and Its Rivals

L

Land border
The line where one country meets another on land. Unit 1: Asia and Where China Sits
Latitude
How far north or south of the equator a place is, in degrees. Unit 2: China's Land and People

M

Master plan
The overall layout of a large site: where buildings, roads and open space go, before each building is designed. Unit 8: The City of Hangzhou
Municipality
Here, a city run like a province, reporting straight to the national government. Unit 3: How the Country Is Organized

O

Output per person
A place's GDP divided by its population; a rough measure of how rich it is. Unit 6: Hangzhou Among China's Cities

P

Peer city
A city of similar size and rank, used for comparison. Unit 7: Hangzhou and Its Rivals
Photovoltaic (PV)
Panels that turn sunlight directly into electricity. Unit 8: The City of Hangzhou
Plateau
A large area of high, fairly flat land. Unit 2: China's Land and People
Port
A city with a harbor where ships load and unload trade goods. Unit 4: History in Four Turning Points
Prefecture-level city
A city that also governs the countryside around it. Unit 3: How the Country Is Organized
Private company
A business owned by people or families, not by the government. Unit 6: Hangzhou Among China's Cities
Province
A large division of the country, like a US state. Unit 3: How the Country Is Organized

R

Region
A group of countries that share a part of a continent. Unit 1: Asia and Where China Sits
River basin
All the land whose rain drains into one river. Unit 2: China's Land and People

S

Services
Work that sells skills or help instead of goods: shops, banks, software, tourism. Unit 7: Hangzhou and Its Rivals
Special economic zone
An area with looser rules for trade and foreign investment. Unit 4: History in Four Turning Points

T

Tidal bore
A wave that runs up a river when a strong tide comes in. Unit 8: The City of Hangzhou
Tier
A rank. A "first-tier" city is one of the country's top cities. Unit 5: The City Map of China
Time zone
An area that sets its clocks to the same hour. Unit 2: China's Land and People
Trade
Goods a country sells abroad (exports) plus goods it buys from abroad (imports). Unit 1: Asia and Where China Sits

W

Wholesale market
A market that sells goods in bulk to shops and traders. Unit 6: Hangzhou Among China's Cities
World Heritage site
A place UNESCO lists as important to all humanity. Unit 8: The City of Hangzhou

Y

Yuan
China's currency. In 2025 one US dollar bought about 7.14 yuan. Unit 5: The City Map of China
