Hangzhou Among China's Cities

Hangzhou is the capital of Zhejiang, a province built on private family businesses, and a home for internet and AI companies.

In this unit you will learn
  • what makes Hangzhou's province, Zhejiang, different
  • Hangzhou's size and wealth, by the numbers
  • how fast Hangzhou connects to its neighbors
  • the big events and companies Hangzhou is known for
Words to know
Private company
A business owned by people or families, not by the government.
Output per person
A place's GDP divided by its population; a rough measure of how rich it is.
Wholesale market
A market that sells goods in bulk to shops and traders.

6.1 Zhejiang, the province of private business

Zhejiang had 67.01 million people at the end of 2025 and an economy of 9.45 trillion yuan, 4th among China's provinces. What sets it apart is who owns the businesses.

In 2024 private companies were over 90% of all businesses in Zhejiang. They gave over 80% of the jobs and produced about two-thirds of the province's economy.

Did you know?

Yiwu, a Zhejiang city, has a wholesale market with about 75,000 stalls. Factories around it made nearly two-thirds of the world's Christmas decorations (2017 figure).

6.2 Hangzhou by the numbers

At the end of 2025 Hangzhou had 12.70 million residents, 85% of them in urban areas. Its economy was 2.30 trillion yuan, up 5.2% on the year, the 8th largest of any Chinese city.

Worked example

Is Hangzhou richer than the rest of its province?

  1. Divide the economy by the people to get output per person.
  2. Hangzhou: 181,732 yuan per person, about $25,400.
  3. Zhejiang as a whole: 141,418 yuan per person, about $19,800.
  4. 181,732 ÷ 141,418 ≈ 1.29.

The average Hangzhou resident produces about 29% more than the provincial average.

Key numbers
  • 12.70 million residents at the end of 2025
  • 85% of them in urban areas
  • 5.2% growth of the economy in 2025

6.3 Neighbors by train

Hangzhou is close to several big cities. The fastest trains from Hangzhou East take about 45 minutes to Shanghai Hongqiao, with more than 270 trains a day. Ningbo is about 48 minutes away, Suzhou about 1 to 1.5 hours (45 minutes on the newer line from Hangzhou West), and Nanjing 1 to 1.5 hours.

Key numbers
  • 45 min fastest train to Shanghai Hongqiao
  • 270+ trains a day on that route, about one every 4 minutes across the day
  • 48 min to Ningbo

6.4 Big events

  • The G20 summit, a meeting of the leaders of 20 major economies, came to Hangzhou on 4–5 September 2016. The light show on the river towers and the evening performance on West Lake (Unit 8) both began for it.
  • The 19th Asian Games ran from 23 September to 8 October 2023, with about 12,400 athletes from 45 countries and regions competing in 481 events. Esports was a medal sport for the first time.
Key numbers
  • 12,400 athletes at the 2023 Asian Games
  • 45 countries and regions
  • 481 events, esports among them for the first time

6.5 Companies you may have heard of

CompanyWhat it makes
AlibabaOnline shopping (Taobao) and cloud computing
GeelyCars; owns most of Volvo Cars and just over half of Lotus; about 4.1 million vehicles in 2025
NetEaseVideo games, including Marvel Rivals, and music streaming
Game ScienceThe video game Black Myth: Wukong, which sold over 10 million copies in its first four days in 2024
DeepSeekAI chatbots
UnitreeRobot dogs and humanoid robots

In 2025 people started calling six young Hangzhou companies the "six little dragons": DeepSeek, Unitree, Deep Robotics (four-legged robots), Game Science, BrainCo (devices that read brain signals) and Manycore (3D interior design software). Hangzhou is also home to the headquarters of 9 companies on the Fortune Global 500 list of the world's largest companies.

Did you know?

Black Myth: Wukong is based on Journey to the West, a classic Chinese novel about a monkey king.
