A short course in 10 units

Hangzhou in Context

Asia, China, its great cities, where Hangzhou fits among them, and the university being built there. Written at about a 10th-grade level for the Buro Happold lighting team, as background to the Tsientang University project.

Start Unit 1: Asia and Where China Sits →

How to use this guide

Each unit is one page. Read them in order: the guide starts with the whole continent and zooms in, one step at a time, until it reaches the streets of Hangzhou, the new university being built there, and the universities it will be measured against. Every unit opens with what you will learn and the words to know, and ends with a few questions to check yourself. Tap "Show answer" under a question to see the answer.

Look for three kinds of boxes. Did you know? boxes hold a surprising fact. Worked example boxes walk through a calculation step by step. Look for it in Hangzhou boxes point to something you can see in the city.

Every number comes from a source listed at the bottom of its unit. Figures are for the end of 2025 unless a unit says otherwise. Money is given in yuan, China's currency, with US dollars, euros and pounds in parentheses, converted at the rates of 3 November 2026 (one dollar = 6.67 yuan, one euro = 7.45 yuan, one pound = 8.93 yuan), whatever the year of the figure.

Where the project is

Hangzhou, capital of Zhejiang province, about 45 minutes by fast train southwest of Shanghai.

Contents

  1. UNIT1

    Asia and Where China Sits

    China is in East Asia, on the world's biggest continent, and it touches 14 countries by land.

  2. UNIT2

    China's Land and People

    China is high and dry in the west and low and wet in the east, so almost everyone lives in the east.

  3. UNIT3

    How the Country Is Organized

    Provinces hold cities, and cities hold districts and counties, which is why a Chinese city can include farms and hills.

  4. UNIT4

    History in Four Turning Points

    China's capital moved many times over 2,100 years. Hangzhou had its turn about 800 years ago.

  5. UNIT5

    The City Map of China

    Most of China's economy sits in three coastal city regions, plus a few giant inland cities.

  6. UNIT6

    Hangzhou Among China's Cities

    Hangzhou is the capital of Zhejiang, a province built on private family businesses, and a home for internet and AI companies.

  7. UNIT7

    Hangzhou and Its Rivals

    Hangzhou is usually compared with Suzhou, Chengdu, Wuhan and Nanjing, cities of similar size that make their money in different ways.

  8. UNIT8

    The City of Hangzhou

    Hangzhou is a river city with a lake at its heart: the old town wraps around West Lake and the new business district lines the Qiantang River.

  9. UNIT9

    Tsientang University

    A bottled-water fortune is building a new research university in the tea hills southwest of the city, designed to disappear into a forest.

  10. UNIT10

    The University Map

    Tsientang will compete for students and professors with universities in the city, the province, the country and the rest of Asia. Here is who they are.

About this guide. Hangzhou in Context is a study guide for the Buro Happold lighting team working on the Tsientang University project: background on Asia, China, Hangzhou, the university and the universities it will be measured against, so that everyone on the team, whether or not they travel, shares the same picture of the context. It is not a Buro Happold publication and not a project deliverable. It is drawn from published sources, the architect's design material and what the team already knows from working on the project, and nothing in it is confidential.

Facts were checked against the sources listed at the end of each unit as of the dates shown. Statistics change every year, rankings have newer editions, and the campus design will keep developing, so treat every figure as a snapshot. Money is converted at one fixed rate, given on the contents page. Photos are credited to their sources and are here for study. Corrections and questions to Josh Spitzig.
